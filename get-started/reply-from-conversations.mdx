When a shopper is handed to your team and Gorgias is not the handover destination, you can reply by email from **Activity > Conversations**. Replies stay in the same email thread, and shopper replies come back into the same conversation in Asa.

<Note>
Reply from Conversations needs a **Starter** plan or above. Free plans still receive handover emails, but you reply outside Asa. See [Plans and pricing](/resources/plan-pricing).
</Note>

<Steps titleSize="h4">

<Step icon="inbox" title="Open a handover conversation">

Navigate to **Activity > Conversations** in the sidebar.

Use the tabs across the top:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All conversations** | Every conversation Asa has had |
| **Handover** | Conversations handed to your team |

Open a handover thread. On the conversation you will see the shopper's name and email when they submitted the handover form, plus the issue they described.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/all-conversations-view.png" alt="Conversations inbox showing the conversation list and Handover tab" />
</Frame>

Handover rows use amber status badges as the thread progresses:

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Handover** | Escalated and waiting for your team |
| **Taken over by human agent** | Someone on your team has already replied from Asa |
| **Closed** | Marked closed after a human reply |

</Step>

<Step icon="reply" title="Reply by email">

In the sticky bar at the bottom of the conversation, click **Reply**.

Type your message in the composer (`Type a reply…`). You can format text from the toolbar. Send with the send button or **Shift + Enter**.

Asa emails the shopper on the same thread. Their reply lands back in this conversation — you do not need to leave Asa or check a separate mailbox.

<Tip>
If **Reply** is disabled, the shopper email is missing on the ticket. If you see an upgrade prompt instead, your plan does not include inbox email reply yet.
</Tip>

</Step>

<Step icon="paperclip" title="Add attachments (Growth)">

On **Growth**, you can attach files to a reply:

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Per file | **1 MB** |
| Files per reply | Up to **3** |
| Total size per reply | **3 MB** |
| Types | Images, PDF, Word (`.doc` / `.docx`), CSV |

Starter can reply with text and formatting, but attachments stay locked until you upgrade.

</Step>

<Step icon="tag" title="Insert a product or discount (Growth)">

On **Growth**, the composer toolbar also includes:

| Control | What it inserts |
| --- | --- |
| **Insert product** | A storefront link to a Shopify product from your catalog |
| **Insert Discount Code** | A redeemable Shopify discount code (codes only — automatic discounts without a redeem code are not listed) |

Use these when the shopper needs a specific SKU or a code to check out. They are optional — plain text replies work on Starter and Growth.

</Step>

<Step icon="check" title="Mark closed or reopen">

After you (or a teammate) has replied from Asa, the conversation toolbar shows **Mark closed**.

- **Mark closed** — hides the reply composer and marks the handover **Closed**.
- **Reopen** — brings the thread back so you can reply again from the composer.

The composer stays hidden while the handover is closed. Reopen from the toolbar when you need to continue.

</Step>

</Steps>

## How do you get notified?

| Channel | Who gets it | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | The handover contact on every plan | New handover attention email. On paid plans that can reply from Asa, the email notes you can reply from your Asa inbox and links **Open conversation**. |
| **Browser notifications** | Optional, per person and browser | OS alerts when a conversation is handed over, or when a shopper replies on a handover thread |

To turn on browser notifications, open the **bell** on the Conversations page, click **Enable**, then choose:

- **A conversation is handed over to your team**
- **A shopper replies on a handover conversation**

You can turn them off anytime from the same menu.

## When do you reply in Asa vs Gorgias?

| Handover destination | Where you reply |
| --- | --- |
| **Email Contact** (no Gorgias) | **Reply** from Asa Conversations — email thread stays in Asa |
| **Gorgias** | **Open in Gorgias** on the conversation — pick up the ticket in Gorgias. Asa does not send inbox email replies for Gorgias handovers |

If Gorgias ticket creation fails, Asa falls back to **Email Contact** so the shopper still has a path to support. See the [Gorgias integration guide](/integrations/gorgias).

| Optional browser notifications | — | When helpdesk reply is on | When helpdesk reply is on |
