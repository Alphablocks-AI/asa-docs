Asa uses outcome-based pricing: start free, upgrade when you need more conversations, knowledge sources, and intelligence. Every paid plan includes a **7-day free trial**. No setup fees or long-term contracts.

For live prices and the full comparison matrix, see [asa.so/pricing](https://asa.so/pricing).

<Info>
All prices are in **USD**. The website may show **Limited time** rates for Starter and Growth.
</Info>

## Plan overview

| Plan | Price (per month) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Trying Asa on your store |
| **Starter** | $49 | Shopping assistance and frontline support |
| **Growth** | $199 | Getting shopping, sales, and support done right |

### Free

For trying Asa on your store.

- **50** conversations / month
- **50** knowledge sources
- Shopify auto-training, files, URLs, Macros
- Self-serve support

### Starter

Everything in Free, plus:

- **500** conversations / month
- **250** knowledge sources
- Google Drive and Notion integrations
- Review integrations (Judge.me, Yotpo, Klaviyo Reviews)
- Advanced behavior, tone, and response controls
- Context-aware product guidance and personalized recommendations
- Reviews and AI summaries
- Basic upsell, cross-sell, and proactive nudges
- Session-only shopper memory
- Email support and immediate escalation notifications
- Basic conversation analytics, event tracking, and insights

### Growth

Everything in Starter, plus:

- **2,500** conversations / month
- **500** knowledge sources
- Advanced appearance customization
- Granular behavior, tone, and response controls
- Recommendations with past-purchase signals
- Advanced upsell, cross-sell, and proactive nudges
- Long-term shopper memory
- Priority email + Slack support
- Advanced inbox, analytics, event tracking, and business insights

## How conversations are counted

Each shopper typically counts as **one conversation per billing month**, even if they send many messages. That one-to-one mapping holds in almost every case.

When a single shopper appears as more than one conversation, it is usually because of one of these situations:

1. **Separate threads** — The shopper has one conversation escalated to your team and another resolved by Asa in the same month (for example, they return after support with a new question).
2. **Session resets** — Browser session or local storage changes on the shopper's device — a new device, cleared cookies, or private browsing — can occasionally start a fresh conversation before we can link it back to the same person.
